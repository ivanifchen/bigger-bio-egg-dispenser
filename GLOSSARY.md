# Bigger bio egg dispenser

A 16-egg, roughly 395 mm remix of pymata's Bio Egg Dispenser, sized for a 16 in fridge shelf: a
lattice cage that sits front-to-back on the shelf. You load eggs at the top front, they roll back, turn down and roll forward to
where you take them out.

## The dispenser

**Dispenser**:
The whole assembled cage: a Back half and a Front half joined at the Seam.
_Avoid_: holder, rack, rolling rack

**Original**:
pymata's 10-egg, 242 mm Bio Egg Dispenser that this project remixes.
_Avoid_: reference model, base model

**Level**:
One straight run of Rails that holds a single row of eggs. The Dispenser has two: the Upper level
and the Lower level.
_Avoid_: tier, row, deck, shelf (Shelf is the fridge's)

**Upper level**:
The Level eggs are loaded onto; it slopes down toward the back.

**Lower level**:
The Level eggs are taken from; it slopes down toward the front.

**Rail**:
One of the two parallel round rods a Level's eggs roll on, lying crosswise.
_Avoid_: rod, track, runner

**Side guide**:
A continuous strip along each end of the eggs on a Level that keeps them crosswise; together the two set the
Channel width.
_Avoid_: wall, fence, bumper

**Channel width**:
The clear width between a Level's two Side guides.
_Avoid_: cage width, inner width

**Egg pitch**:
The length one egg takes up along a Level; egg count times Egg pitch sets the Dispenser's length.
_Avoid_: egg spacing, slot

**Slope**:
The angle a Level's Rails drop over their length, in degrees.
_Avoid_: incline, pitch, grade

**Turn**:
The back end of the Dispenser where eggs leave the Upper level, drop briefly and land on a curved
ramp that feeds the Lower level.
_Avoid_: U-turn, chute, drop, drop point

**Skew**:
An egg rotating out of crosswise, so its long axis is no longer square to the Rails; a skewed egg
jams. Eggs skew because they are fatter at one end and roll in a curve.
_Avoid_: turning (that is the Turn), twisting, tipping

**Loading lip**:
The upturned front end of the Upper level's Rails, where eggs are put in.

**Exit cradle**:
The upturned front end of the Lower level where the front egg rests until taken.
_Avoid_: dispenser end, scoop, tray

## The cage

**Lattice**:
The organic network of Struts that forms the Dispenser's sides, top and bottom around the Rails.
_Avoid_: mesh (that is the file), web, frame, voronoi

**Strut**:
One round branch of the Lattice between two Nodes.
_Avoid_: bar, beam, branch, tube

**Node**:
The thickened, blended point where Struts meet.
_Avoid_: joint (that is the Seam's), junction, knot

## The split

**Back half**:
The half of the Dispenser that holds the Turn.

**Front half**:
The half of the Dispenser that holds the Loading lip and the Exit cradle.

**Seam**:
The plane at mid-length where the Back half and Front half meet.
_Avoid_: joint, split line, splice

**Contact**:
A place where something crosses the Seam and the two halves touch. Grown has 14: four Pin
bosses, four Rails, four Side guides and the two waist lines; nothing else comes near the Seam.
_Avoid_: joint, crack, join

**Pin boss**:
A thickening on a frame line, one at each corner of the Seam, that holds a Pin's hole; the
Lattice Struts that would have crossed the Seam run into it instead.
_Avoid_: knuckle, socket, lug

**Seam band**:
Stretch's flat ring round the cage at the Seam, carrying its Pin bosses. Grown has none.
_Avoid_: collar, flange, joint ring

**Pin**:
A separate D-shaped peg that pushes into a boss on both Seam bands to hold the halves together.
_Avoid_: peg, dowel, key

**Fit coupon**:
A small test print of two Seam band pieces and Pins, used to check the Pin fit before a half.
_Avoid_: test piece, calibration print

## Prototypes

**Stretch**:
The prototype made by lengthening the Original's mesh, bending the Levels back to their Slope and
adding Side guides and V ties.
_Avoid_: hack, remix (the whole project is the remix)

**Grown**:
The prototype whose Lattice is generated from scratch around the Rails, Side guides and Turn.
_Avoid_: generated, custom, own design

**V tie**:
A pair of Struts from a Node in the middle of the top or bottom out to both sides, opening
toward the front; it joins the two sides so they don't sway while printing.
_Avoid_: chevron, cross brace, tie bar

## Fit

**Shelf depth**:
The clear front-to-back depth of the fridge shelf with the door closed; it caps the Dispenser's
length.
_Avoid_: fridge depth
