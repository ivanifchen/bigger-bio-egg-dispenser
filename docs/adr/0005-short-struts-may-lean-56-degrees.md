# Short Lattice Struts may lean 56 degrees, past the 45-degree rule

The shared design rules cap unsupported overhangs at 45 degrees. Held to that, every Strut in Grown ran within 45 degrees of the print direction, and the Lattice came out as long thin vein-like cells that didn't look like the Original. The Original's look comes from roughly round cells, which need Struts leaning much further. So in Grown a Strut no longer than 24 mm may lean up to 56 degrees from vertical (34 degrees above horizontal), longer ones stay within 45, and short Struts square to the print direction are allowed as bridges of up to 18 mm between Nodes that print. Every Node still needs a printable Strut reaching it from below, and the check reports overhanging surface (2-4% against the Original's 5%). Expect a little sag under the steepest short Struts, as the Original's printers report.
