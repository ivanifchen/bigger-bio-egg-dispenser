# Longer, not taller: two Levels at shelf depth, printed as two halves

The Original holds 10 eggs as two Levels of 5 in 242 mm, already 242 mm tall when printed on its back end (the X2D builds to 260 mm). To hold more, we keep two Levels and make the Dispenser as long as the fridge shelf allows. The shelf is 16 in (406 mm) deep, and the Dispenser sits front-to-back, so length = 8 eggs × a 47 mm Egg pitch (room for XL) + about 17 mm of Turn and Exit cradle ≈ 395 mm, for 16 eggs. A part that long fits neither upright nor flat (the bed diagonal is about 360 mm), so the Dispenser prints as a Back half and a Front half joined at a Seam at mid-length. If the finished model comes out over about 400 mm, we drop to 7 eggs per Level rather than shrink the Egg pitch.

## Considered Options

- **A third Level at the Original's length (15 eggs, one print):** no Seam, but taller, and it is the designer's paid 15-egg version in all but name.
- **Two rows side by side:** doubles capacity in one print, but needs a second, more complex egg path and a much wider footprint.
