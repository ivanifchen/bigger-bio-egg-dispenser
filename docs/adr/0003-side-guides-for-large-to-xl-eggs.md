# Continuous Side guides, 64 mm apart, for Large to XL eggs

The most common complaint about the Original (about 20 of its 340 written comments) is eggs Skewing and jamming, mostly at the Turn and mostly with smaller eggs, plus XL eggs sticking. Measured from its mesh, the Original's side Struts sit about 62 mm apart, but between them the sides are open Lattice cells: an egg, being fatter at one end, rolls in a curve, its end drifts into a cell and catches. We add a continuous Side guide along both ends of the eggs on every Level and through the Turn, 64 mm apart: 2 mm more than the Original so XL eggs (about 60-62 mm long) roll, while a 57 mm Large egg can Skew only about 10°. Medium and Small eggs still fit but may Skew; a different egg size means changing the Channel width and Egg pitch, not the Slope. Slope stays at the Original's 2.2°, since the comments ask for more push, never less.
